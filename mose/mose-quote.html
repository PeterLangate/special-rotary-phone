Introduction

Section 1The opportunity

Many organisations want the protection that Microsoft 365 sensitivity labels provide — encryption, access control, and a clear audit trail — but adoption stalls. The labels exist in the tenant, yet sensitive material stays scattered across hundreds of SharePoint sites and personal OneDrive accounts, unlabelled and unprotected.

MOSE — Move to Security — closes that gap. It gives your administrators a guided way to create protected areas and to find and consolidate sensitive content into them, so a label is applied automatically the moment content arrives. What is normally a manual, error-prone project becomes a repeatable, auditable workflow.

In one sentence: MOSE lets you set up a secure SharePoint area with a sensitivity label, search the whole tenant for the words that describe your sensitive content, and move what you approve into that area — where it is protected on arrival.

Solution

Section 2What MOSE does

MOSE delivers two capabilities that work together. An administrator uses them in sequence: first define where protected content should live, then find the content that belongs there.

1Create a secure area

MOSE provisions a new SharePoint site and applies a chosen sensitivity label as its default. From then on, everything added to that area is labelled and protected automatically — no per-file action needed. Create one area per category, e.g. HR, Finance, Legal.

2Discover & move

MOSE searches every SharePoint site and OneDrive in the tenant for the words that describe sensitive material. Results are presented for review; the administrator decides what to move into the secure area, where the label is applied on arrival.

A worked example

An HR administrator sets up a secure area named HR with the Confidential · HR label. They then search the tenant for HR, salaries, bonuses, incentives, severance. MOSE returns every matching item across all sites and OneDrive accounts.

Folder-level intelligence: if a folder contains a match — for example a document with the word bonus — MOSE suggests the whole folder as a move candidate, so related material is not left behind. The administrator can accept the folder, or drill in and pick individual items.

The administrator reviews the suggestions, selects what should be protected, and clicks Move to security. MOSE relocates the approved content into the HR area, the sensitivity label is applied automatically, and every action is written to an audit log.

The user stays in control. MOSE never moves anything on its own — it discovers and suggests; a person approves. Items under legal hold or other constraints are flagged and left in place.

Delivery & scope

Section 3How we deliver

Architecture

MOSE is delivered as two connected parts that share the same proven code:

  • SharePoint dashboard — a SharePoint Framework (SPFx) web part where administrators create secure areas, run searches, review candidates, and trigger moves.
  • Azure Function companion — a scheduled background service that re-runs saved searches to catch new matching content over time, so protection stays current without manual effort.

Built on proven components

Rather than building from scratch, MOSE assembles Langate's existing, production-hardened shared components — the same libraries that power our established Microsoft 365 products. Every capability MOSE needs is already solved and battle-tested:

  • Secure-area provisioning — creating the SharePoint site and setting its default sensitivity label
  • Tenant search — finding matching content across SharePoint sites and OneDrive
  • Content movement — relocating approved items into the secure area and applying the label
  • Configuration & audit — storing settings and a full, tamper-evident activity log

This reuse is the core of the reduced risk and faster timeline. A detailed technical component map is available to your team as a separate addendum under NDA.

Implementation stages

  1. Discovery & assessment — confirm your labels, target areas, and keyword sets; validate tenant permissions.
  2. Deployment — install the MOSE dashboard and Azure Function into your tenant; configure app-only access.
  3. Secure-area setup — provision your initial protected areas and default labels.
  4. Pilot move — run a controlled discovery and move on a limited scope to validate results.
  5. Rollout & handover — full-scale use, administrator training, and documentation.

Security & compliance

Section 5Security & compliance

MOSE is granted broad reach over your content by design — it must be able to search every site and move approved material. We treat that trust as the core of the engagement. This section sets out how that access is scoped, protected, logged, and kept inside your control.

Runs inside your tenant

Both parts of MOSE — the SharePoint dashboard and the Azure Function companion — are deployed into your own Microsoft 365 tenant and Azure subscription. Your content is read, indexed, and moved within your tenant; it is never copied to Langate systems or any third party. Microsoft (as your existing cloud provider) is the only sub-processor. Data residency follows your tenant's region.

Least-privilege access

MOSE runs as a single app registration using app-only access with a certificate credential stored in Azure Key Vault. Only the permissions below are requested; the exact scopes are confirmed and consented during Discovery, and are scoped to the sites in play wherever your policies allow it.

Security & compliance

Safe by construction

Human approves every move

MOSE discovers and suggests; a person selects and confirms. Nothing moves autonomously. High-impact scopes can require a second approver.

Label verified on arrival

The move completes only once the sensitivity label is confirmed applied. If labelling fails, the action is rolled back and reported — never left silently unprotected.

Holds respected

Items under legal hold, retention, or other constraints are detected, flagged, and left in place rather than moved.

Sharing reviewed

Existing sharing links on moved items are surfaced for remediation, so "protected" does not hide a still-open link.

Audit trail

Every action — who, what, source, destination, label applied, timestamp, and outcome — is written to an append-only audit log. Records are retained for an agreed period and can be exported for your compliance evidence.

Discovery is best-effort, not a guarantee. Keyword search finds indexed, text-bearing content; scanned images, unindexed files, and material outside the chosen keywords may not surface. MOSE reduces exposure and makes it auditable — it does not claim to locate every sensitive item in the tenant.
