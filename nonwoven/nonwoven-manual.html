Chapter 2 — Installation

Chapter 2Installation

The dashboard is one self-contained HTML file. It needs no installation, no server and no network.

2.1 Open the dashboard

  1. Copy Dashboard.html to your computer or to a shared folder.
  2. Open it in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome (double-click the file).
  3. Pick your role in the list at the top right - the reports reorder for that role.

2.2 Verify that it works

A working dashboard shows the report grid on the left, today's headlines in the middle and the Claude panel on the right. The figures in the top bar change when you click 24 h, 7 d or 30 d.

Tip: imported files (chapter 3) are kept in the browser you imported them in. Another browser or computer starts without them.

Chapter 3 — Importing data

Chapter 3Importing data

Bring your own Excel or CSV file into the data warehouse. The import wizard reads the file, proposes what every column means, links it to the lines, shifts, grades, customers and fibres already in the warehouse, and builds new reports from it.

3.1 Start an import

  1. Click 📥 Importera in the top bar.
  2. Drop a .xlsx or .csv file on the green area, or click it to choose a file. No file at hand? Click ✨ Prova med exempelfil for a sample of customer complaints.
  3. For a workbook with several sheets, the first sheet that holds data is opened. Pick another one in the 📑 Blad list.

3.2 Check the column mapping

Every column gets a role, a confidence bar and the reason for it. Green means the wizard is sure; amber or grey is worth a look. Change a role in its list - your choice is then marked vald av dig.

3.4 The new reports

Step 4 lists the reports the data supports: each measure over time, each measure per linked dimension, and - when a total is linked to a line or customer - the measure per 100 t of fabric produced. Click 📥 Importera to add them to the dashboard. They follow the period buttons and the filters like every other report.

3.5 Remove an import

Open 📥 Importera again. Under Importerat every import is listed with its row count and time; 🗑 Ta bort removes it and all its reports.

Tip: give the import a short, clear name in step 2 - it becomes the prefix of every report it creates.
